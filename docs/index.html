Venture Capital Firm Structures

Three entities, three jobs: the ManCo runs operations on fees, the GP makes decisions and earns carry, and the Fund holds LP capital. The separation exists so fee income, carry, and investor capital never sit in the same pocket.

Venture Capital Performance

The Power Law: one investment returns more than all others combined. VC isn't a diversification model — it's a concentrated bet, with diversification used to buy more tickets in the same lottery.
