## FI Sprint 4 — Deals
Start: 2026-08-03 · Due: 2026-08-10 · Mentor: Neal Strickberger (GP, Decile Group / Seed Milestone)

Sprint guidance: build a functional deal pipeline, produce a first AI-assisted deal memo, and
begin the outreach working investors do daily. Some information will be hard to obtain and some
outreach won't get responses within a week.

### Set 1 — Understanding Dealflow
Deepen your understanding of the dealflow management process. First, review the six steps of the
dealflow management process, reflected in the titles of the articles at (https://vcl.to/f8a).
Next, choose one aspect of dealflow management you are less familiar with, such as setting
effective first- and second-level filters or executing deals, and write a couple of sentences on
what you want to know or develop in this area. Then, read the relevant DecileGroup.com article
plus at least two other sources to dive into the topic and write a couple of sentences about what
you learned. Lastly, write a couple of sentences on which areas of dealflow you want to explore
further later in the program.

### Set 2 — Ecosystem Mapping
Understand where deals in your niche actually come from. First, re-read your Map Your Niche
deliverable from Sprint 2 and identify five highly successful companies from your niche that have
had a notable exit or late-stage round, writing a bulleted list with each company's name, year,
approximate value, and one sentence describing the company. Next, evaluate whether there are
strong enough examples of venture scale in your niche, or whether you may need to widen the niche.
Then, for each of the five companies, trace their earliest two or three backers (like angels,
accelerators, incubators, or first institutional investor) and write a bulleted list with the
backer, when/how they invested, and a sentence on how the relationship likely formed. Lastly,
contact at least three sources from your research to open a casual, relationship-building
conversation and write a couple of sentences describing your outreach and any initial responses.

### Set 3 — Deal Sourcing
Source active investment opportunities and build your deal pipeline in Decile Hub. First, review
the video on Adding Deals to Hub at (https://fi.co/guides/2721/legacy_show). Next, identify at
least three startups currently fundraising within your niche and add all three to Hub by trialing
multiple methods of adding deals, writing a couple of sentences on which methods you used and
which worked best. Then, contact each of these startups to get in touch for the Deal Memo set
below. Lastly, write a bulleted list of each startup with their name, URL, funding stage, a
one-sentence description, how you found them, and why you think it is a good deal.

### Set 4 — Deal Memo
Create an AI-assisted deal memo grounded in a real founder conversation. First, choose the most
promising startup in your pipeline and interview the founder for a deal memo, which you can offer
to share with them as an asset for their fundraise. Next, review the video on Deal Memos in Hub at
(https://fi.co/guides/2721/legacy_show), and create your own deal memo for the best startup in
your pipeline. Then, when the AI has finished drafting a memo (±10 minutes), revise the draft by
adding your evaluation, correcting incorrect information, and filling in unanswered questions, and
share the revised memo with your working group. Lastly, provide constructive feedback on at least
two peers' memos, focusing on the evaluation more than the quality of the deal, and write a couple
of sentences on where AI helped with deal evaluation and where humans must be involved.

### Set 5 — Assignment Review
Complete the first program review to continue in the program. First, go through each of your
Sprints 1 through 3 and write a bulleted list scoring each submission on a 1 to 5 with no 3s, with
one sentence of reasoning per Sprint. Next, for any Sprint scored below a 4, go back and improve
the work before submitting, ensuring all links are accessible through an incognito browser.
Lastly, complete the mandatory Venture Review Survey at https://vcl.to/vi-review and write a
couple of sentences summarizing your key learnings from the first half of the program.
