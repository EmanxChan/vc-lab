# Assignment — Your Moat Wedge

**Submitted:** July 2026
**Prompt:** Write 5 venture wedge statements for assistant-AI / AI co-working use cases, name the
exact founder harm each prevents, pick the top 2 and identify the buyer, then write "Wedge Ready."

---

## Part 1 — Five wedge statements

1. We back AI co-workers that run back-office operations for small clinics at pre-seed in the US to
   prevent owner-operators from losing their best hours to insurance paperwork.

2. We back AI agents that capture institutional knowledge as people leave at pre-seed in the US to
   prevent founders from re-buying knowledge they already paid for.

3. We back AI co-working tools for deskless and frontline teams at pre-seed in the US to prevent
   operators from losing good staff to scheduling and communication chaos.

4. We back AI that drafts and files regulated documentation at pre-seed in the US to prevent
   founders from betting the company on a filing a junior hire prepared at midnight.

5. We back AI operations partners for teams under ten people at pre-seed in the US to prevent
   founders from hiring a role they can't afford eight months early.

## Part 2 — The exact harm prevented

1. **Clinic back-office.** The owner spends 12–15 hours a week on claims and prior authorizations.
   None of it is billable. Denied claims go uncontested, so earned revenue gets written off.

2. **Knowledge capture.** A senior departure costs 3–6 months of replacement ramp. The remaining
   staff absorb the gap, get overloaded, and become the next people to quit.

3. **Frontline co-working.** Missed shifts and lost messages drive turnover in roles that are
   expensive to refill, and it burns out the managers who then leave too.

4. **Regulated documentation.** One bad filing means fines, remediation cost, and a stalled launch
   or dead deal. That's a cash and survival event, not an inconvenience.

5. **AI ops partner.** The founder either burns runway on a salary revenue can't support, or skips
   the hire and personally becomes the bottleneck on every process in the company.

## Part 3 — Top two wedges

**AI operations partner for teams under ten people**
The buyer is the founder or first ops hire at a 3–10 person company with revenue but no operational
headcount. They pay early because the alternative is a $90K salary they can't justify yet, and a few
hundred dollars a month against that comparison is an easy yes.

**AI back-office for small clinics**
The buyer is the owner-operator of an independent practice or their practice manager. They pay early
because recovered claims and reclaimed clinical hours are countable in the first month, and this
buyer already pays per seat for billing software.

## Part 4 — Wedge Ready

- **Top wedge:** AI operations partners for teams under ten people
- **Target geo:** United States
- **Stage:** Pre-seed to Seed
- **Founder harm prevented:** Founders either burn runway on an ops hire the revenue can't support,
  or skip it and become the bottleneck themselves. Both cost months and cash they don't have.
