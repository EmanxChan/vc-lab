## Why it matters

The pipeline is the record of every company under consideration and where it sits. It's the
operational artifact that makes a process a process rather than a feeling.

It also produces the numbers that let you improve: how many companies at each stage, how many
advance, and where things die. Without it, you can't tell whether the problem is sourcing or
conversion.

## What it looks like in practice

Each entry needs a stage, a date, a source, and a reason for the current status. The reason
field is what makes it valuable later.

Aging matters. Deals that sit in a stage indefinitely are usually passes that nobody wanted to
write down, and they distort the picture of what's actually live.

## Where it goes wrong

- Keeping the pipeline in your head, which works until it doesn't.
- Letting deals age in a stage rather than recording a pass.
- Not recording the source, which makes it impossible to evaluate channels.
- Tracking only active deals, which discards the anti-portfolio data.
