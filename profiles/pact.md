## Why it matters

PACT is VC Lab's standard for emerging manager fundraising — a short, standardised set of terms
designed to compress the time between an LP saying yes and money actually arriving.

The problem it addresses is real: first-time managers spend enormous amounts of time negotiating
bespoke terms with LPs who are each asking for something slightly different, and the negotiation
itself is often what kills the fund.

## What it looks like in practice

The approach mirrors what standardisation did for early-stage company financing. When both sides
start from a common document, the conversation narrows to the few things that genuinely differ.

For a manager raising a first fund the practical value is speed and credibility — arriving with
standard terms signals that you know what market looks like.

## Where it goes wrong

- Assuming standardisation removes the need to understand the terms.
- Using a standard where an institutional LP expects a fully negotiated LPA.
- Treating speed as the only goal when some terms genuinely warrant negotiation.
- Not checking that the standard terms fit the actual strategy.
