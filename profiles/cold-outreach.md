## Why it matters

Cold outreach is how you reach founders outside your network, which is where most companies are.
Response rates are low, and the ones that respond are often the deals nobody else is looking at.

For an emerging manager without inbound flow it's not optional. It's also the only systematic
correction to the network bias that warm introductions create.

## What it looks like in practice

What works is specificity: evidence you've understood what they're building, a reason you
specifically are relevant, and a short ask. What fails is anything that reads as a template.

Research is the whole job. A hundred well-targeted messages outperform a thousand generic ones
by a wide margin, and the research is also how you find the companies worth writing to.

## Where it goes wrong

- Sending templated messages at volume.
- Leading with your fund rather than with why you understand their problem.
- Reaching out only when a company is already raising.
- Judging the channel on response rate rather than on the quality of what it surfaces.
