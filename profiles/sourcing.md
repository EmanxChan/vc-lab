## Why it matters

Sourcing is the active work of finding companies before anyone else does, and at pre-seed it's
most of the edge. Once a company is running a process, the price is set by competition and the
advantage is gone.

It's also the part of the job that compounds slowest and matters most. Relationships built three
years before a company exists are what produce proprietary access.

## What it looks like in practice

The approaches that work are specific rather than broad: depth in a sector, presence in a
community, a public thesis that attracts the right founders, and relationships with the people
founders talk to before they raise.

Geography still matters more than it should. Being physically present in an ecosystem produces
flow that remote relationship-building doesn't replicate.

## Where it goes wrong

- Sourcing broadly rather than deeply, which produces volume without access.
- Meeting founders only when they're raising.
- Building a public thesis you don't actually invest against.
- Neglecting the people around founders — operators, engineers, community organisers.
