## Why it matters

Term sheet conversion is the share of term sheets issued that actually close. A low rate means
you're losing deals after committing, which is expensive in time and worse in reputation.

It's a direct measure of competitiveness. Founders who take another offer after receiving yours
are telling you something about price, terms, or how you're perceived.

## What it looks like in practice

The causes divide into price, terms, and relationship. Price is the easiest to observe and often
not the real reason — founders frequently choose a lower offer from an investor they'd rather
work with.

Asking directly after a loss is worth doing. Most founders will tell you, and the answer is more
useful than any inference.

## Where it goes wrong

- Assuming losses are always about price.
- Not asking why, which discards the most useful feedback available.
- Issuing term sheets speculatively, which damages credibility when they're withdrawn.
- Tracking the rate without tracking the reasons.
