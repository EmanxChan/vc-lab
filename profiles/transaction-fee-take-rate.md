## Why it matters

The take rate is the single most consequential number in a marketplace, and it's constrained
from both directions. Too low and the business can't fund itself. Too high and both sides start
looking for ways to transact around you.

It also determines what business you're in. A 3% take rate is a payments business that needs
enormous volume. A 25% take rate is a services business that has to justify itself with real
work — trust, insurance, dispute resolution, demand generation.

## What it looks like in practice

Sustainable take rates track how much of the value you actually create. Platforms that only
introduce two parties struggle to hold double digits. Platforms that handle payment, guarantee
quality, resolve disputes, and generate the demand can hold 20–30%.

Watch for leakage: high-frequency, high-trust relationships migrate off-platform fastest. If a
cleaner and a household will work together weekly for years, the second booking is where the
take rate quietly disappears.

## Where it goes wrong

- Benchmarking against a platform that provides far more or far less than you do.
- Raising the take rate to fix unit economics and triggering the leakage that kills them.
- Reporting gross transaction volume as revenue.
- Charging the side that's harder to acquire.
