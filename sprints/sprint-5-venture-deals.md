## My agent-assisted motion

Three agents, one for each stage of the deal lifecycle. Written during Sprint 1 (Thesis Wedge
Selection) and kept here because this is where they actually get built.

**1. Sourcing** — an agent that watches alumni activity across **Plug and Play**, **Grid110**,
and **Material Change** to flag trust-focused founders *before* they start fundraising.
*Why it works:* this is my edge made mechanical. The alumni signal exists whether or not I'm
watching it; the agent means I don't miss it.

**2. Diligence** — an agent that **adversarially tests** a startup's misinformation or trust
product. If a company claims to rebuild trust, the fastest read is to try to break it.

**3. Support** — an agent that routes portfolio founders to alumni mentors and tracks user trust
signals after investment, like churn or complaint spikes.
*Why it works:* trust erosion shows up in support tickets before it shows up in revenue.

## What this sprint should give me
- A repeatable sourcing motion for pre-seed AI founders in the US
- A deal memo template I can write in under 90 minutes
- A market analysis approach that works when there's no revenue to analyze

## Deal memo skeleton (draft — refine during the sprint)
1. **The one-liner** — what they do, in the founder's words
2. **Why now** — what changed in the world to make this possible
3. **Team** — why *these* people, specifically
4. **Market** — size, and more importantly, shape
5. **Thesis fit** — does it actually rebuild trust, or am I stretching?
6. **The bet** — the single thing that has to be true
7. **Terms & ownership** — entry price, check size
8. **Anti-memo** — the strongest case against, written honestly
